BrainRush Privacy Policy

BrainRush: Brain Puzzle Games

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BrainRush: Brain Puzzle Games ("BrainRush", "we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

BrainRush does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, phone number, or other registration details.

The app stores gameplay-related information locally on your device, such as scores, levels, stars, coins, game history, and preferences.

Our advertising and consent-management services may process certain information, including device identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions, app activity, diagnostics, and consent choices, depending on your device, region, and applicable settings.

2. Advertising Services

BrainRush uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information to provide, measure, and improve advertising services.

For more information about how Google handles data, please visit: Google Privacy Policy .

3. Consent Management

BrainRush uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), where applicable, to provide privacy and advertising consent choices. Available choices may depend on your location and applicable regulations.

You can review Google's information about advertising and privacy at: Google AdMob Privacy & Messaging .

4. How We Use Information

Information may be processed for the following purposes:

  • Saving your game progress and preferences on your device.
  • Displaying and measuring advertisements.
  • Managing advertising consent where applicable.
  • Maintaining app functionality, security, and performance.

5. Data Sharing

Gameplay progress stored locally is not transmitted by BrainRush to our own servers.

Information processed by Google Mobile Ads and related services may be shared with Google and its service providers or advertising partners as necessary to provide advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and related services.

6. Data Storage and Security

Gameplay information is stored locally on your device. Advertising-related information may be processed by Google according to Google's privacy and security practices.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

BrainRush is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us.

8. Your Choices

You can manage available advertising consent choices through the consent message shown in the app, where applicable. You can also manage advertising settings through your device and Google's available privacy controls.

Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored gameplay data according to your device's normal app-data removal behavior.

9. Third-Party Services

BrainRush uses third-party services provided by Google. Their processing of information is governed by their own privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
